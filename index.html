About Me

The mind and hands behind the easel.

Pooja Patkar | Pooluxe Art

Welcome! I am an independent visual artist exploring the quiet interplay of light, emotion, and storytelling across my work. Through my Light Art, I celebrate how natural brilliance touches diverse forms—bringing pieces that feel abstract up close, yet resolve into luminous realism from afar as jewelry, chandeliers, and forms.

My Realistic Human Figures focus closely on the face, capturing intimate warmth, rich skin tones, and subtle emotional gestures.

Alongside these, my Illustrations bring a warm, expressive charm to life, ranging from festive holiday moments and quiet landscapes to whimsical silhouettes and character portraits. Across every collection, each canvas is an invitation to pause, look closely, and experience the radiance woven into every detail.
